# Core Principles of Meta Programação Filosófica do Gheto

## The Four Foundational Rules

### Rule 1: Infinite Love [~]
There is no mathematical limit to love in the educational process. Love here means:
- Genuine care for the learner's wellbeing
- Patience with the learning process
- Recognition of inherent worth and potential
- Commitment to growth without coercion

**Application**: Create spaces where learners feel emotionally safe to make mistakes, ask questions, and explore ideas without judgment.

### Rule 2: Never Forget the Question [/]
The dupla (learning pair) must always maintain curiosity and inquiry. This means:
- Every answer should generate new questions
- Questions are more valuable than answers
- The process of inquiry is more important than conclusions
- Doubt and uncertainty are tools, not obstacles

**Application**: Begin each session by identifying what questions are driving the learning. End by noting what new questions have emerged.

### Rule 3: Student Controls Learning [o]
The learner must have agency in their educational journey:
- Self-directed pace and focus areas
- Choice in learning methods and materials
- Freedom to challenge and reshape content
- Responsibility for their own growth

**Application**: Create structures that support self-direction while providing guidance when requested.

### Rule 4: Self-Care First [=]
Never love anyone more than yourself, especially regarding the end of your own suffering:
- Educators must maintain their own wellbeing
- Boundaries are essential for sustainable helping
- Self-awareness prevents projection and burnout
- Personal healing enables better support for others

**Application**: Regular self-reflection, seeking support when needed, and recognizing when to step back.

## The Dupla Framework

Rather than hierarchical teacher-student relationships, Spectro uses "duplas" - collaborative partnerships where both parties contribute to learning. This framework:

- Eliminates power imbalances that create trauma
- Recognizes that everyone has knowledge to contribute  
- Creates mutual responsibility for the learning process
- Honors the full humanity of all participants

## Anti-Asylum Education

Traditional schools often function as mental institutions, creating trauma rather than healing. Spectro advocates for:

- Healing-centered practices
- Recognition of systemic oppression's impact on learning
- Validation of diverse ways of knowing
- Integration of emotional and intellectual development

## Love-Based Methodology

At its core, this approach is "fundamentally based on love." This means:

- Starting from a place of care and connection
- Assuming positive intent and inherent worth
- Using relationship as the foundation for learning
- Transforming fear-based educational practices
